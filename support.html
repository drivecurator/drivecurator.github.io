Safety: set up the app before you drive and keep your eyes on the road. Drive Curator is built to play hands-free; please don't operate it while driving.

How it works

What is Drive Curator?

A live podcast about wherever you are. It uses your location to find true stories about the places you're passing — history, legends, local heroes, quirky events, food traditions and more — and narrates them out loud, one after another, like a podcast.

How do I start?

Open the app, tap Start exploring, set the radius and the interests you like, and tap Start narrating my drive. Allow location when iOS asks. No account or sign-up is needed. To hear stories about somewhere you're not, type a city, state or ZIP in the box at the bottom of the home screen and tap Go.

Where do the stories come from?

Stories are written by AI from information about real places and then read aloud. We ask the AI to tell only things it can confirm, but AI can make mistakes. If something sounds wrong, tap Report on the story card so we can pull it. Tap Look it up to search the topic yourself, or Tell me more for a deeper dive.

What do the controls do?

Thumbs up/down tell us which stories you like. The radius control on the player changes how far away the app looks for places (1–50 miles). Use the pause and skip buttons on the player, or your car's steering-wheel controls over Bluetooth or CarPlay audio.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Stories and studio voices are generated and delivered online, so you need a data connection. Free device voices read each story on your phone, but the story itself still has to be downloaded first.

The voice sounds robotic, or I want a different voice.

On the home screen under Narrator you can choose a studio voice (Rex, Ara or Eve) or a free voice built into your phone via Device voice. You can add better-sounding free voices in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices. A limited number of studio-voice stories are free to try; Curator+ unlocks them all.

Dark mode

Turn on Dark mode in the Narrator section of the home screen.

Subscriptions (Curator+)

What does Curator+ include and cost?

Curator+ includes all three studio narrators (Rex, Ara and Eve), longer and richer stories, and The Pulse (real local events happening in the next few days). It costs $5.99 per month or $39.99 per year (prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy).

How does billing work?

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in iOS: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Drive Curator and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also use the App Store app (tap your profile picture › Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. You keep Curator+ until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple ID, open the Curator+ screen (tap one of the premium toggles on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Family Sharing / using on multiple devices

Curator+ is tied to your Apple ID. Use Restore purchases on any other device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Location settings

The app can't find me, or says location is blocked.

Open iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, make sure Location Services is on, tap Drive Curator and choose While Using the App or Always. Also check that Precise Location is on. Then reopen the app and tap play. Or just type a city, state or ZIP on the home screen — that needs no GPS at all.

Stories stop when my screen locks or I switch apps.

Choose Always for location in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Drive Curator. This lets the app keep following your drive in the background. With While Using, it works only while the app is open on screen. Also make sure Low Power Mode is off and that you haven't force-quit the app.

What is my location used for?

Only to find stories near you and to estimate miles driven. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is sent and to whom.
